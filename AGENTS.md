# Mars Wind Lab — source and data boundaries

- Implement project-specific analysis independently from published scientific methods and authorized inputs.
- Do not access, restore, copy, translate, port, or derive results from the former internship/professor folders, scripts, reports, eigenfunctions, or their Trash copies. Do not recreate their reader or reconnect their paths.
- Existing extracted data expressly authorized by the project owner may be retained. Keep each public dataset's provenance, attribution, and license; the code's MIT license does not apply to external data.
- Public visibility alone is not permission to copy code, redistribute a dataset, or publish an adaptation. Establish the license or explicit permission for the intended use before incorporating a new source; otherwise exclude it pending clarification.
- Keep proprietary or separately licensed tools external. Use MCD software only through an authorized installation and its official interface, respecting its scientific-use, attribution, notification, and non-distribution terms.
- Never send permission requests or usage notifications to third parties without the owner's explicit instruction.
- Recompute project results from permitted inputs. Validate numerical implementations against analytic cases or authorized published references, never private internship outputs.
- See `docs/SOURCE_POLICY.md` for the source audit and unresolved permissions.
