# Earlier experiments

These studies explain why the current six tests need controls. Their saved results and interactive settings remain available, but the [current report](../DISCRIMINATING_TESTS.md) is the entry point for conclusions.

## Spatial association is not regional prediction

The earlier [map workshop](../EXECUTED_EXPERIMENTS.md?view=maps) compares dispersed held-out cells with omitted longitude wedges. Performance changes when nearby training information disappears. [Test 1](../DISCRIMINATING_TESTS.md#test-1--surface-age-as-a-predictor-of-the-magnetic-map) extends that question to geological epochs and hemisphere indicators. Shift controls diagnose spatial alignment; they are not automatically valid permutation p-values.

## A rock records a time window

The [recording benchmark](../EXECUTED_EXPERIMENTS.md?view=recording) and [thermal workshop](../THERMAL_EXPERIMENT.md) show how cooling and reheating select parts of a field history. [Tests 2 and 4](../DISCRIMINATING_TESTS.md#test-4--coherence-under-a-reversing-dynamo) apply this idea to accepted conductive histories. The [detection workshop](../EXECUTED_EXPERIMENTS.md?view=detection) separates seeing a signal from identifying its history; this helps interpret the amplitude and surface comparisons in Tests 3 and 5.

## A good fit is not an ancient age

The [laboratory control](../EXECUTED_EXPERIMENTS.md?view=laboratory) includes known contaminated material. The [paleointensity exercise](../DICHOTOMY_PHYSICS.md?view=paleomagnetism) varies recording efficiency. Both constrain how strongly source requirements in [Test 3](../DISCRIMINATING_TESTS.md#test-3--the-magnetization-budget-cylinder-equivalent-scenario) can be interpreted as rock properties. Neither adds a new planetary verdict.

## Geometry and reference terrain matter

The [regional pilot](../PILOT_STUDY.md) explores crater interiors and reference rings. Its contrasts depend on sampling choices. [Test 5](../DISCRIMINATING_TESTS.md#test-5--ground-truth-for-the-orbital-model) adds a different limitation: an orbital model does not resolve all local ground fields. The [crustal support workshop](../DICHOTOMY_PHYSICS.md?view=crust) prepares the density–thickness question now treated by the full inversion in [Test 6](../DISCRIMINATING_TESTS.md#test-6--crustal-thickness-with-laterally-variable-density).

Amplification and boundary-restoration workshops remain exploratory controls, not independent confirmation of a six-test verdict. The archived methods below keep their own reproduction commands, input records and earlier conclusions.
