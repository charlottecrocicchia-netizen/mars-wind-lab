Inspect the geology comparison

Outside means are reweighted to the interior's surface map-unit proportions, using only units present on both sides. This changes the comparison population. It does not control deep lithology, alteration age or impact heating.

Inspect the crater-depth diagnostic

Published-method transfer using the local 4 ppd MOLA grid and fixed catalog rims. The band shows the middle half of azimuthal elevations, not an error bar. The original joint inversion has not been reproduced.

What the mineral markers mean

Each marker is a 2° cell containing at least one MOCAAS mineral detection. A blank area is unknown. These points are context only and are not used as a wet/dry variable in the calculation.

CRISM and OMEGA detections are unioned by class. The coordinate convention has not been independently validated at native deposit scale; no precise spatial association is inferred.
