REGIONAL PILOT · 04 / 05

A comparison you can inspect.

The source data, calculation choices and limits of the test are part of the result.

Choose the cases before calculating the contrasts

The protocol defines four primary craters and a separate Ladon window, selected from published hydrothermal candidate regions. Newton and Copernicus are part of the same regional setting. Selection is literature-led and exploratory, not random or preregistered.

Four geometries use the IAU/USGS Gazetteer, MDIM 2.1, planetocentric east-positive coordinates. Ladon's catalog entry is flagged “Misidentified” in the author-hosted revised catalog; it is used only for sensitivity, not as a validated crater.

Recalculate the same observable at three altitudes

Evaluate the full degree-134 Langlais et al. (2019) magnetic model at 130, 150 and 400 km above its 3393.5 km reference sphere. Use the magnitude of the three-component field at each point, then area-weight means with cos(latitude). A 0.5° sampling grid is numerical quadrature, not independent observations or true 30 km magnetic resolution.

Four direct evaluations reproduce the existing rounded atlas to within 0.000465 nT. This is a numerical consistency check using the same model, not independent geophysical validation.

Magnetic model: Langlais et al. (2019)

Transfer a published topographic diagnostic

Sample the 4 ppd MOLA elevation grid along rays every 30° out to 2 R. Take the median over azimuth, then calculate depth from the minimum within 0.2 R to the median at 1–1.2 R; divide by the diameter.

This follows the radial recipe in Mittelholz et al. (2025), with fixed catalog rims, a different working grid and no visual rim adjustment. It is a method transfer, not an exact numerical reproduction. The paper's joint density–magnetization inversion and gravity selection have not been rerun. Its regularization also favours related density and magnetization patterns.

Published method and study context · MOLA archive

Make the reference comparison explicit

The baseline ratio is mean |B| inside 0.8 R divided by mean |B| in the ring 1.2–2 R. The gap avoids directly mixing the rim into both samples. The surrounding ring is a local reference, not a proven unaltered control.

For a second comparison, retain surface map units present inside and outside, then reweight their outside means to the interior's unit proportions. Report how much interior area remains. Map units are broad surface classifications, not precise ages or deep magnetic lithologies; they can also be consequences of the event being studied. This is descriptive standardization, not a causal adjustment.

We separately exclude interiors of other catalog craters ≥150 km from the reference ring, omitting entries labelled misidentified. This does not remove their ejecta, intrusive heating or buried impacts.

USGS geologic map · Versioned crater catalog

Try to break the interpretation

For every window, run 81 combinations: three altitudes × three inside radii (0.6, 0.8, 1 R) × three reference rings (1.2–1.8, 1.2–2, 1.5–2.5 R) × three radius factors (0.9, 1, 1.1). Repeat the map-unit calculation for each combination. At the baseline, omit each of eight 45° sectors in turn.

The displayed envelopes are ranges over chosen analysis settings, not confidence intervals. Altitudes share model coefficients; nearby grid points are correlated. We do not use pixel counts as replicate counts and do not compute p-values or mechanism probabilities. No physically calibrated model exists here to score on held-out regions.

OBSERVATION COVERAGE AUDIT

Why there is no “water causes weak magnetism” test

The acquired MOCAAS archive contains ten mineral rasters. Its paper discusses coverage maps, but no usable survey/exposure mask was found in the downloaded bundle or the project's listed global downloads. A general instrument footprint would still not encode dust, burial, detection sensitivity or which observations were analysed.

We therefore use occupied 2° cells as presence-only context. They never define wet and dry groups. Surface signatures do not automatically sample deep magnetic sources.

Carter et al. (2023), coverage and detection limits · MOCAAS downloads

Reproduce the complete saved study

From the repository root, with the base observation inputs already acquired:

pip install -e '.[observations,research,test]'
python scripts/data/acquire_pilot.py
python scripts/data/build_pilot.py
python scripts/research/render_pilot.py
pytest -q -m 'not integration'

On a new installation, first follow the base acquisition instructions in the README (several GB). The pilot build performs no network requests. Output records source, protocol and code SHA-256 hashes. Raw third-party archives remain local.
