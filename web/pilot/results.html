THE PHYSICAL IDEA TO CARRY FORWARD

Model the recording history before inferring the ancient field.

The data support this as a research strategy, not yet as a demonstrated geological history. Copernicus and Huygens provide comparatively stable contrasts to explain. Newton tests sensitivity to the surface-unit mixture; Schiaparelli tests spatial scale and missing comparable terrain.

The next discriminating evidence is a dated link between a magnetic carrier and an alteration or heating event, coupled to regional source geometry and adequate observation coverage. A model should then predict a region withheld from fitting. Adding unconstrained histories to explain every map would not constitute a test.
