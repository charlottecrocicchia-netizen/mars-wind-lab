Samuel et al., 2023 Liquid material alone is not sufficient; energy transport, composition and stable stratification matter. Do not treat comments as the paper's conclusion.

The discussion also links an Fe–S–H immiscibility model. That is a follow-up lead, not a validated explanation adopted in this project. It needs comparison with newer core-structure constraints. Older thread dates matter: a 2023 discussion cannot assess the 2025 inner-core interpretation or 2026 tidal tomography.

For further discovery, prioritize scientist-authored conference discussion, instrument-team documentation, NASA/ESA archive help and references supplied by participants. Trace every scientific claim back to an original paper, dataset or mission document before adding it to an evidence matrix. Popularity and confident wording are not quality measures.
