What normalization changes

Two archived scripts used |U|² + 2|V|² and |U|² + ℓ(ℓ+1)|V|² respectively. This ratio quantifies the difference between the integrals; it cannot determine the correct harmonic convention on its own.

05 / TRACEABILITY

Start from the data,
preserve the conventions.

The engine calls CALL_MCD, the official Mars Climate Database 6.1 routine installed locally. It joins lower and upper atmospheric fields and converts vertical coordinates.

Raw files contain pseudo-altitude, which must not be treated as geometric altitude. EOFs are variability modes, not absolute wind fields.

Available scenario: climatology, average solar EUV. Other scenarios and Mars years require their complete MCD files.

Quantities and conventions

u is positive eastward, v northward, w upward. Azimuth α is measured clockwise from north. Displayed shear is √[(∂u/∂z)² + (∂v/∂z)²]. Maps use a ±1 km vertical step; profiles use their own grid.

Global means are weighted by spherical cell area. Missing values are excluded and valid areas renormalized. Atlas arrows show projected direction; their length is normalized.

What this version supports

Explore consistent fields, compare seasons, locate jets and shear, export data, and investigate the sensitivity of a simplified acoustic column.

What still needs validation

Global coupling kernels, archived U/V eigenfunction conventions, acoustic losses, boundary conditions and ground excitation still need validation. Non-Hermiticity alone does not establish attenuation. This version provides no SEIS inversion or energy partition.
