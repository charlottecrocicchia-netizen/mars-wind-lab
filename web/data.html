How to read this map

Drag to zoom, double-click to reset, or choose a crater from the menu. Crosses are candidate source craters from published studies; they are not confirmed launch sites. The dashed curve is the published Andrews-Hanna dichotomy boundary, not the equator.

The display grid is 2°. Topography is a block average of MOLA data; magnetic fields use the full degree-134 model. Crustal maps show four examples from one interior-model family. Surface geology is mapped at 1:20 million. None gives the composition of a deep meteorite source directly.

What does this inventory cover?

15 entries from Weiss et al. (2025), SI Table S1, plus the NWA 7034 contamination study. This is a published paleointensity compilation, not a complete census of Martian meteorites. The chronological catalogue contains 94 sample records and has a different scope.

Published paleointensity uncertainties have mixed definitions and often omit calibration systematics. Bounds remain bounds. We do not fit a dynamo lifetime from these numbers. Paired stones are not independent Martian regions.

A marker means at least one detection inside a 2° cell. An empty cell means unknown, not dry. Spectral coverage, dust, burial and exposed rock all affect what can be seen.

Resolution, overlap and source

The November 2023 MOCAAS release contains ten class maps: five each from OMEGA and CRISM. Every nonzero native pixel contributes to a presence cell; small deposits are not discarded by nearest-neighbour downsampling. Instrument detections are unioned within a class, not counted as independent deposits.

The project publishes coarse numerical occupancy summaries. Original GeoTIFFs remain local; the source page does not state an explicit reuse license. A survey/exposure mask is still needed for a valid comparison with non-detection areas.

Coverage still to extend

This is a first integrated collection, not all Mars observations. Further work includes calibrated surface magnetometer comparisons, GRS elemental maps, full-resolution mineral analysis, survey/exposure masks for mineral detections, additional laboratory records and uncertainty propagation through thermal and chemical histories.
