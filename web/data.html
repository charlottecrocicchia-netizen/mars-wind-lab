How to read this map

Drag to zoom, double-click to reset, or choose a crater from the menu. Crosses are candidate source craters from published studies; they are not confirmed launch sites. The dashed curve is the published Andrews-Hanna dichotomy boundary, not the equator.

The display grid is 2°. Topography is a block average of MOLA data; magnetic fields use the full degree-134 model. Crustal maps show four examples from one interior-model family. Surface geology is mapped at 1:20 million. None gives the composition of a deep meteorite source directly.

Dating conventions & original tables

Sample crystallization uncertainties in Herd Data S1 are 2σ; the group summaries in S3 use 1σ. NWA 7034 clast ages are distinct from breccia lithification. The downloaded JSON retains the separate original age compilation and the NWA 7034 isotope table with each dating system.

A MEASURED SIGNAL IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY AN ANCIENT FIELD

What happened to the rock after it arrived?

These NWA 7034 paired stones were affected by collectors’ hand magnets, according to Vervelidou et al. (2023). Their demagnetization curves are useful for checking contamination. They cannot establish the ancient field at a candidate source crater.

Scope and units

Alternating-field demagnetization changes the measured remanent moment. The graph displays the laboratory values as recorded, with method codes and treatment amplitude. It is not a paleointensity reconstruction. MagIC locations in this dataset refer to collection on Earth, not launch on Mars.

Coverage still to extend

This is a first integrated collection, not all Mars observations. Further work includes calibrated surface magnetometer comparisons, GRS elemental maps, CRISM mineral products, other meteorite laboratory records and uncertainty propagation through thermal histories.
