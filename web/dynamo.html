START HERE / FIRST CALCULATIONS COMPLETED

Can different field histories leave the same signal?

Use one shared recording model to compare a steady field, reversals, interruptions and shutdown. Then test what remains detectable at different heights. Original recording, detection, regional-transfer and laboratory benchmarks are now available. They expose ambiguities; they do not select a Martian dynamo history.

Explore the executed experiments → · Explore the existing thermal experiment
Four distinctions to keep in mind
  • A weak or null magnetic signal does not by itself prove that the dynamo was off.
  • An axial field can reverse polarity. Geometry and reversal history are separate choices.
  • Present-day interior structure does not directly date ancient dynamo conditions.
  • A laboratory material constraint does not set a universal dynamo shutdown date.

T1Early permanent shutdownChronology hypothesis · Executed building block · full test pending
Proposition to examine
The dynamo stopped permanently around 4.1–4.0 Ga.
Proposed test
Search for younger, independently dated remanence attributable to a global field; compare active-field and no-dynamo predictions under the same recording and detection model.
Required inputs
Magnetization ages, local crustal-field estimates, carrier histories and calibrated detection limits.
What would discriminate?
A secure younger global-field record would contradict permanent shutdown at the proposed age.
What could mislead us?
A weak or null local field cannot establish shutdown: erasure, burial, cancellation and insufficient resolution remain alternatives.
Available project work
Executed an idealized detection benchmark; it does not establish a shutdown date or a younger global-field record.

T3Long-lived activity with reversalsChronology and recording hypothesis · Executed building block · full test pending
Proposition to examine
A reversing dynamo could leave weak basin fields through cancellation during cooling.
Proposed test
Predict the same basins at several altitudes for reversing, stable, intermittent and extinguished histories; include noise and mineral-source uncertainty.
Required inputs
Cooling and acquisition kernels, source mineralogy, excavation geometry, field histories and measurement response.
What would discriminate?
A shared observation model must reproduce both strong surrounding fields and weak basin interiors, including resolvable spatial structure.
What could mislead us?
The reversal rate is a model parameter, not a measured Martian time series. Mantle excavation alone is not tested under an absent dynamo in the 2025 abstract.
Available project work

T4Distinct ALH 84001 recording episodesSample chronology constraint · Executed building block · full test pending
Proposition to examine
Separate magnetic components may record different field directions during the rock’s history.
Proposed test
Build a component-level ledger of carrier, petrographic context, direction, laboratory treatment and event-age association before comparing episodes.
Required inputs
Published component measurements, age links, uncertainty and alteration/handling history; reuse permission for any data import.
What would discriminate?
