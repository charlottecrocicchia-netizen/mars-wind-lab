A RESEARCH PROJECT ABOUT MARS

Why does Mars have
two different faces?

Lower plains in the north, higher terrain in the south: this is the Martian dichotomy. This site brings together evidence to investigate its origin and the history preserved in rocks.

WHERE DOES THE PROJECT STAND?

Evidence organized.
The origin still open.

Maps and initial calculations help test the robustness of observations. They do not yet select a unique origin of the dichotomy.

The comparison and chronology are sourced syntheses. A first thermal experiment now checks two controlled temperature histories. Confronting regional models with shared constraints remains the next step.

A few terms to help you navigate
Crust
The outer rocky layer of Mars.
Mantle
The rocky interior beneath the crust.
Remanent magnetization
The memory of an ancient magnetic field preserved by minerals.
Ma and Ga
Millions and billions of years before the present.
