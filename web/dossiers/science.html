READING DOSSIER · 28 September 2026

Scientific context

Mars's topography, crustal thickness and magnetic field describe different parts of the same history. The useful question is which histories satisfy these observations together. The six current tests investigate recording and preservation, cooling, source strength and density; they do not yet select a unique origin of the dichotomy.

Start with what is measured

Spacecraft measure magnetic fields and gravity. MOLA supplies surface shape. Crustal thickness, source depth and magnetization are inferred through models. Surface geological epochs describe exposed terrain; they do not automatically date the deeper magnetic source. The atlas makes these layers available together, while Results states the assumptions behind each inference.

Follow the magnetic record

A rock can acquire thermal, chemical or shock remanence, then lose or replace part of it. A surviving signal depends on the carrier, grain properties, field, cooling history and later alteration. The recording workshop illustrates this filtering. The laboratory workshop demonstrates why a straight demagnetization path alone supplies neither an acquisition age nor an ancient field strength.

Connect the interior to the surface

Density and thickness trade off in gravity inversions. Heat production, basal heat flux and crustal growth influence when minerals can record a field. Impacts, volcanism and fluids can change both the rock and its record. Test 6 tests declared density scenarios; Tests 2 and 4 examine a limited family of conductive histories. None includes all these processes simultaneously.

Keep competing explanations visible

Different magnetic signals could reflect different ancient fields, different recording materials or different preservation histories. Several mechanisms may operate together. The scenario comparison retains the broader alternatives; the current report asks narrower questions that our available data and implementations can answer.

The original notes below preserve their citations and qualifications. They are background to the current report, rather than additional independent results.
