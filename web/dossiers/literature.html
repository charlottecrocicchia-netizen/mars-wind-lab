READING DOSSIER · 28 September 2026

Literature method

Every scientific statement should have both a source and a reading status. Finding a title does not mean reading a paper, and consulting selected sections does not constitute a full methodological review.

Search, then assess

The project combines targeted searches, citation trails, public archive documentation and reference books. The source catalogue is searchable by topic and reading depth. Its discovery candidates are leads awaiting assessment. The search log and citation queue preserve the earlier search trail.

Record what was consulted

Separate metadata, abstracts, selected full-text sections and any genuinely complete reading. Note which claim a passage supports, what it cannot establish, and whether supplementary methods or data remain inaccessible. The interdisciplinary source ledger also includes books and cross-disciplinary references; it overlaps the catalogue, so their totals must not be added.

Distinguish reading from reuse

An accessible article can inform an original analysis without granting permission to redistribute its figures, data or code. Dataset provenance, attribution and applicable terms belong beside each input. The project code's license does not replace external source terms. The input provenance view and the six-test source ledger identify the materials used in calculations.

Check novelty separately

“New to this project” is not “never tested.” A new combination of existing methods requires a targeted literature check before any priority claim. The next-test protocols identify relevant prior work and leave novelty unestablished. Forum discussions are useful search leads, not evidence for physical claims.

This collection is an evolving, AI-assisted research synthesis. It does not claim an exhaustive systematic review or complete readings of every listed article and book. The archived notes below document earlier scope choices and outstanding checks.
