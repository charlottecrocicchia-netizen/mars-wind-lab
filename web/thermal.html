FROM AN OBSERVATION TO A PHYSICAL TEST

Can an old magnetic
record stay cool?

A rock can cool, then heat up again. Follow temperature through a crustal column and see when later heating rules out an older magnetic record.

WHAT THIS EXPERIMENT TESTS

Two controlled histories. One thermal exclusion rule.

Both columns start identically. One cools progressively; the other receives an imposed heat pulse after 1 billion years. These are illustrative histories, not reconstructions of northern or southern Mars.

Below a mineral's ordering temperature, magnetic recording may be possible. It can still fail because of relaxation over time, alteration, shock or the absence of a suitable field.

NUMERICAL CHECKS

3. Check the calculation before interpreting it

The solver is tested against an exact steady geotherm, an exactly decaying temperature perturbation, energy balance and resolution refinement. These verify the calculation; they do not validate a Martian geological history.

Assumptions, units and excluded processes

A 50 km solid column conducts heat vertically. Its surface is fixed at 220 K (−53.15 °C). Conductivity is 3 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹, density 2,900 kg m⁻³ and heat capacity 800 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹. All are illustrative constant choices.

Upward basal heat flux decays from 45 toward 15 mW m⁻² with a 1,000 Myr time constant. Uniform volumetric heating starts at 0.04 µW m⁻³, with an effective 2,000 Myr half-life. Basal flux is not surface heat flux; the heating law is not an isotope inventory.

The heat pulse is an instantaneous, tapered Gaussian increment, peaking at +500 K at 25 km depth. It is a controlled perturbation, not an impact or intrusion model. The full mathematical definition, mesh and source hashes are in the protocol and method note.

There is no crustal growth, lateral heat transport, latent heat, fluid alteration, grain-scale relaxation, shock or dynamo history. No orbital field is predicted. Endmember ordering temperatures are not measured local mineralogy or blocking-temperature spectra.

Reproduce or download this experiment

This is a saved run. Opening the page does not run a new simulation. From the repository root, after the base installation:

python scripts/thermal/build.py
python -m pytest -q tests/test_thermal.py

No external download is required. The outputs contain synthetic temperatures and exclusions only.
