01 / AN IDEA WE CAN CHALLENGE

Two different magnetic archives.

WORKING HYPOTHESIS · UNTESTED

An early contrast in crustal structure may have created different histories of heating, cooling and magnetic preservation. Part of today's magnetic contrast could reflect how the rocks recorded a shared dynamo.

We test this baseline first because it needs fewer assumptions about ancient field asymmetry. It has not been selected by a joint fit. Impact and internal-growth origins remain open.

THE DISCRIMINATING QUESTION

Was the deep southern crust magnetized as it formed, or after it was reheated?

Both histories can produce strong anomalies. Source depth, carrier minerals and event timing must be tested together.

Change the assumption. Change the prediction.

Open a model to see its requirement and a possible failure. These are regional endmembers, not measured probabilities.

A · SHARED DYNAMOInherited archiveEarly acquisition, long survival

Requires: deep magnetic sources that survive later heating, burial and impacts.

Would fail if: every allowed thermal history erases the proposed inherited record at its required depth.

Test: compare source-depth ensembles with retention histories for plausible carriers.

Acquisition & survival tests ↗
B · SHARED DYNAMORebuilt archiveLater acquisition after reset or growth

Requires: new minerals or cooling crust recording a field after a heating or emplacement event.

Would fail if: no allowed field-active interval overlaps the required acquisition window, or cancellation makes the signal too weak.

Test: align event ages, cooling duration and a range of dynamo histories.

Why recording duration matters ↗
C · ASYMMETRIC DYNAMOInheritance + field contrastPreserved archive, different ancient field

Requires: a viable inherited archive and sustained hemispheric field contrast during acquisition.

Would lose its justification if: field asymmetry adds no predictive improvement once recording and preservation are constrained.

Test: predict withheld regions with equal treatment of carrier and thermal uncertainty.

A coherence constraint ↗
D · ASYMMETRIC DYNAMORebuilding + field contrastLater recording in a different field

Requires: both later acquisition and a coherent field asymmetry during that interval.

Would fail if: no recording window survives. A stronger field cannot rescue a rock that records nothing.

Test: pass the thermal and chronology gates before comparing extra field parameters.
